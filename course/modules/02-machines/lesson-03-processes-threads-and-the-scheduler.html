Module 2 · Lesson 2.3

Processes, threads, context switching and the scheduler

Lesson 2.2 established that the kernel is the referee that shares one machine among many programs. This lesson zooms into the busiest part of that referee: how it shares the single most contended resource — the CPU — among many processes at once, switching between them thousands of times a second so quickly that six programs feel simultaneous on one core.
